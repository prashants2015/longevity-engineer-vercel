# Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

This notice explains, in plain language, what personal data Longevity Engineer collects, why, who it goes to, and how you control it. It is separate from our [Terms of use](http:///terms).

## 1\. Who we are

Longevity Engineer is a personal health and nutrition assistant you use through WhatsApp and a private web page ("Your File"). It is run by Longevity Engineer India ("we", "us").

## 2\. What we collect

We only collect what the service needs. Some of it is health data, so we ask for your consent before collecting any of it.

**Account details**

- Your WhatsApp phone number and the name you give us.  
- If you join the waitlist: your city, goal, phone type and wearable.

**What you tell us or send us**

- Your goals, and why they matter to you.  
- Age or date of birth, sex, height, weight and body-fat figures.  
- Food preferences, dislikes, allergies and intolerances, and who cooks for you.  
- Supplements you take (doses are stored but not shown back to you).  
- Photos of meals and menus, and what you ate.  
- Voice notes. We keep the transcript.  
- Body-composition reports and glucose sensor (CGM) files you send.  
- Blood reports, once we start reading them.  
- Anything you paste in from another AI assistant ("import memory").  
- Your chat messages with us.

**What you choose to connect**

- **Apple Health** (through the Health Auto Export app): sleep, workouts, steps, heart rate and active energy.  
- **Google Calendar**: only when you're busy, never meeting names, guests or descriptions.  
- **Swiggy**: your saved delivery addresses, the orders we place for you, and your order history.  
- **Places**: the location pins you share for home and office. We store the city and area in your profile. Delivery addresses stay in your Swiggy account.

**Automatically**

- On the web: one sign-in cookie, and basic technical logs (time, page, errors). No advertising or tracking cookies.  
- Records of your consent: what you agreed to, which version, when, and any withdrawal.

We don't collect diagnoses or prescription medicines. If you mention them, we don't store them.

## 3\. Why we use it

- To work out your daily targets and tell you what to eat next.  
- To log meals from your photos, messages and voice notes.  
- To read your watch data, calendar and location, so a suggestion fits your day.  
- To place food orders on your Swiggy account, when you ask or under autopilot rules you set.  
- To send the messages you've switched on: morning brief, meal picks, follow-ups, celebrations and weekly debrief.  
- To show you your history in Your File, and to make share images only when you ask.  
- To keep the service safe: spotting red flags, enforcing limits and fixing problems.

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

## 4\. Consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give by replying "I agree" in WhatsApp after reading this notice. You can withdraw it at any time by sending "withdraw consent". We then stop processing and sending messages, and offer to delete your data. Withdrawing doesn't affect anything done before it.

Some of these services process data outside India. We only use countries the Government of India has not restricted, and we choose providers with strong security.

## 7\. How long we keep it

- While your account is active, we keep your data so the service works.  
- If you send "delete my data", we delete it, except the minimum we must keep by law (for example, your consent record).  
- If you don't use the service for \[12\] months, we tell you at least 48 hours in advance, then delete your data.  
- Technical logs are kept for up to \[90\] days.

## 8\. Your rights

- **See your data**: open Your File (send "my file"), or ask us for a copy. \[Export arrives in the next update.\]  
- **Correct it**: send "that's wrong:" followed by the correction.  
- **Delete it**: send "delete my data".  
- **Withdraw consent**: send "withdraw consent".

- **Complain**: contact our grievance officer (section 1). If you're not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## 9\. Security

Connection keys (such as Swiggy and Google tokens) are encrypted. Sign-in links work once and expire after 15 minutes, and sessions use secure cookies. Access to your data is limited to what the service needs. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

## 10\. Children

Longevity Engineer is for adults. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18\. If we learn a user is under 18, we stop and delete their data.

## 11\. Not medical care

Longevity Engineer is a coaching tool, not a medical service. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

## 12\. Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we'll tell you in WhatsApp and, where needed, ask for your consent again.
