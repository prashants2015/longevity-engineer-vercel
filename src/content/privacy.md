## 1. What our service is

Longevity Engineer is a personal health and longevity assistant that works over WhatsApp, with an optional iOS app. It gives guidance on nutrition, training, sleep and recovery. It is **not** a medical service and does not diagnose or treat any condition. It is free during our beta.

## 2. Data we collect

**Information you give us**
- Your WhatsApp phone number and profile name.
- Messages you send to the assistant: text, photos (for example, meal photos) and voice notes. Voice notes are transcribed to text so the assistant can understand them.
- Health and lifestyle information you share, such as goals, age, height, weight, diet, exercise, sleep, symptoms, conditions, medications and lab results.
- Health context you choose to import from ChatGPT (by pasting text, sharing a link, or uploading a data export). We show you what we found and only save it after you confirm.
- Files you send us on WhatsApp, such as lab or blood-test reports, a glucose (CGM) export file from LibreView, or a training plan from your coach (as a photo, PDF or text).
- Details you provide when you book a call (name, email, chosen time).

**Information from connected services, only if you choose to connect them**
- **Apple Health / Apple Watch (iOS app):** with your permission, our iOS app reads the Apple Health data types you choose to grant through Apple HealthKit. Depending on what you allow, this can include activity (such as steps and active energy), workouts, heart data (such as heart rate and heart rate variability), sleep, body measurements (such as weight and body fat), vitals, nutrition, mobility, and cardio fitness (VO2 max). We only read the types you grant, and you can change this any time in iOS Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices.
- **Health Auto Export:** if you set up the third-party Health Auto Export app to send your Apple Health data to us, we receive the data types you configure in that app.
- **Google Calendar:** if you connect your Google Calendar, we read your upcoming events (such as times and titles) so the assistant can plan meals, reminders and bedtime around your day. You can disconnect it any time.
- **Swiggy (Food, Instamart, Dineout):** if you connect a Swiggy service, we access what is needed to place and track your orders and bookings: the link to your Swiggy account, your saved addresses, your cart, and order or booking details. Each Swiggy service requires your separate consent, and we never place an order or booking without your final, explicit confirmation.

**Information collected automatically**
- Technical data needed to run the service, such as message timestamps, delivery status, and basic logs and error reports.
- On our website, see [Cookies and website analytics](#cookies) below.

## 3. How we use your data

We use your data only to provide and improve the service for you, specifically to:
- reply to your messages and give you personalised guidance;
- log your meals, activity and progress, and keep **Your File** up to date;
- send you check-ins and reminders (you can reply **STOP** to opt out of these proactive messages);
- help you place orders through Swiggy, when you have connected it and confirmed the order;
- arrange calls you book with us;
- keep the service secure, fix problems and respond to your requests;
- comply with the law.

We do **not** sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. We use AI model providers under their business (commercial or API) terms, which restrict how they may use customer data, including limits on using it to train their models.

## 4. Apple HealthKit data

Data obtained through Apple HealthKit is used only to provide health and fitness guidance to you within Longevity Engineer. In line with Apple's rules:
- we read HealthKit data only with your explicit permission;
- we never use HealthKit data for advertising or marketing, and never sell it to advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers;
- we never share HealthKit data with third parties except service providers who help us run the service (and are bound to protect it) or where you have asked us to;
- we do not store HealthKit data in iCloud.

## 6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored outside India, in **Singapore**, where our database (Supabase) and our application server (Railway) are hosted. Some providers, such as Meta, Anthropic, Groq, Google, Vercel and Cal.com, may also process data in other countries, such as the United States. ## 7. How we protect your data

We treat health data as sensitive. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access is limited to what is needed to run the service, and links to Your File are sent to you privately by the bot. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable security measures to protect your data, and if a personal data breach occurs we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

## 8. How long we keep your data

We keep your data for as long as you use Longevity Engineer. If your account has no activity for **12 months**, we will message you in advance and then delete your data. If you ask us to delete your data, we do so as described [below](#delete-data). Backups are kept for up to **30 days** and then overwritten. We may keep a minimal record (for example, your consent record, or that a deletion request was completed) where the law requires it.

## 10. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act and this policy, you can:
- **Access and correct your data.** View and edit everything we hold about you in **Your File**, which you open through a secure link the bot sends you on WhatsApp. You can also ask us by email.
- **Withdraw consent.** Disconnect Apple Health (in iOS Settings), Google Calendar, or any Swiggy service at any time, and stop proactive messages by replying **STOP** on WhatsApp. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing done before you withdrew it.
- **Delete your data.** See the next section.
- **Raise a grievance.** Contact us (details below). If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
- **Nominate someone.** You may nominate a person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so, by emailing us.
